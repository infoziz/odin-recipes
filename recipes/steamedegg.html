Steamed egg recipe

Description

Easy and healthy method to make hard steamed egg. Note: this recipe uses induction cooktop and induction pot.

Ingredients

  • None.

Steps

  1. Add 1.5L of water to pot(or any amount enough to steam long)
  2. boil till rolling boil(2000w)
  3. turn down to 1300w and add steaming basket with egg inside.
  4. close lid and set timer 8~10min
  5. While waiting, set up ice bath.
  6. Once times up, put the eggs in the ice bath immediately,calmly and carefuly.
