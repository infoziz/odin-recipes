Chicken Recipes

Description

Very delicious and homemade Airfried chicken recipes. Simple and easy.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Smoked paprika
  • Onion powder
  • Garlic powder
  • Salt
  • Black pepper powder
  • Olive oil or Avocado Oil

Steps

  1. Defrost chicken before cooking(if needed)
  2. Cut to eatable pieces.
    eg:
    • if whole leg, then cut the leg and the big piece.
    • if wings, cut the wings.
  3. Get a bowl and mix all ingredients except chicken together, using a spoon
  4. Then mix the chicken into the bowl using a glove(if you don't want to get your hands dirty)
  5. Marinate 30 mins. This is for full dissolving salts and ingredients into the chicken.
  6. Preheat 5mins Airfryer. Then add 22~24min of airfrying.
    Additional: You can flip them halfway, to make sure all sides are fried equally.
